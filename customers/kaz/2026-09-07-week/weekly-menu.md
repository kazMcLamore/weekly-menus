### 🍗 DISH 1: Carne con Chile Colorado (Beef in Red Chile Sauce)
**Source:** https://www.isabeleats.com/chile-colorado-recipe/ (4.79★/346 votes, first-gen Mexican American, family staple)  
**Cross-check:** https://www.thekitchn.com/chile-colorado-recipe-23576524 (test-kitchen version, 2.5 hr braise, no flour needed)  
**Why this recipe:** Isabel Orozco-Moore's chile colorado is 4.79★ from 346 votes — a family staple recreated authentically (guajillo + ancho + árbol). Beef chuck is one of the cheapest braising cuts and this freezes for 3 months (source-confirmed). The Kitchn's test-kitchen version corroborates the method AND confirms the flour coating is unnecessary — which makes this naturally gluten-free as-is.

**Original servings:** 6 | **Scaled to:** 10 portions (1.65×), 8 assigned + 2 buffer  

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown
| Nutrient | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Calories | ~330 kcal |
| Protein | 37g (45%) |
| Carbs | 15g (18%) |
| Fat | 13g (35%) |
| Fibre | 5g |

*Source card: 328 kcal / 37P / 15C / 13F per serving. Plate with arroz rojo (~115) + papas con chorizo (~165) ≈ ~660 kcal.*

#### Macro Calculation Summary (USDA)
Beef chuck 150g raw → ~113g cooked ≈ 265 kcal / 27P / 14F; chile sauce + broth ≈ 50 kcal / 6C; flour coating ≈ 20 kcal / 3C (or GF flour). Total ≈ 330 kcal / 37P / 15C / 13F (P 45% / C 18% / F 36%). With rice + papas: ~665 kcal / ~43P / ~57C / ~18F — a lean, high-protein plate.

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes 10 portions, 1.67× the source)
**Ingredients:**
- 1.35kg beef stew meat / chuck roast, cut into ½-inch chunks (source 2 lb for 6)
- 3 tbsp **cornstarch or GF flour** (replaces the source's all-purpose flour coating — The Kitchn confirms no thickener is needed; use just enough to dust)
- 12 guajillo chiles, stemmed + seeded
- 3 ancho chiles, stemmed and seeded
- 1–2 chiles de árbol, stemmed (heat — use 1 for medium)
- ¾ yellow onion (½ in the sauce, ¼ for serving)
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1.5 tsp dried oregano
- 1.5 tsp kosher salt (reduced ~20% from 1.5 tbsp-equivalent per portion; taste at the end)
- ¾ tsp ground cumin
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 3.75 cups beef broth (GF)
- 1½ bay leaves
- ¼ tsp black pepper

**Method:**
1. Rehydrate: chiles + onion in a pot, cover with water, boil 1 min, off heat, covered 20 min (do NOT keep boiling — bitterness).
2. Blend softened chiles + onion + 1 cup chile water + oregano + 1 tsp salt + garlic + cumin until smooth. Set aside.
3. Toss beef with GF flour/cornstarch, remaining salt, pepper. Sear in batches in the oil 5 min per batch until crusty.
4. Return all beef + red sauce + broth + bay leaf. Boil, reduce, cover, **simmer 45 min**.
5. Uncover, taste for salt, simmer 15 min more until the sauce reduces and thickens. Discard bay.

**Storage:** Fridge 5 days; **freezer 3 months** (source-confirmed). Reheat covered (chile splatters and stains).

#### Scaling Notes
- Scaled 1.67× from a 6-serving recipe → 10 portions.
- **GF:** the author's own note — use tapioca starch or white rice flour instead of AP flour; or skip the coating entirely (The Kitchn's version skips it entirely and relies on reduction — either works; cornstarch dust is our pick).
- **Spices scale ~linearly** (a saucy braise in a large volume); salt reduced slightly; taste before adding more.
- The red sauce freezes 6 months on its own — make extra and freeze for future weeks (source tip).

### 🍗 DISH 2: Chicken Chile Verde (Pollo en Salsa Verde)
**Source:** https://www.isabeleats.com/chicken-chile-verde/ (4.62★/34 votes)  
**Cross-check:** https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/220993/chicken-chile-verde/ (tomatillo-chile sauce, same architecture)  
**Why this recipe:** The chicken version of chile verde — boneless thighs instead of pork shoulder means ~1 hour total instead of 3, leaner, cheaper, and it freezes 3–4 months (source-confirmed). Naturally GF. Smoky broiled tomatillo-poblano-jalapeño sauce = exactly Kaz's "bold and flavorful" brief. Thursday's medium-cook dish.

**Original servings:** 8 | **Scaled to:** 10 portions (1.25×), 8 assigned + 2 buffer  

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown
| Nutrient | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Calories | ~290 kcal |
| Protein | 34g (48%) |
| Carbs | 10g (14%) |
| Fat | 13g (43%) |
| Fibre | 2g |

*Source card: 241 kcal/29P/9C/10F per serving (8 servings from 2.5 lb thighs). Our 10-portion cut ≈ same per-portion size. Plate with arroz rojo (~115) + lentejas (~150) ≈ ~555 kcal.*

#### Macro Calculation Summary (USDA)
Chicken thigh 140g cooked ≈ 290 kcal / 34P / 19F (raw ~175g/serving → cooked ~105g... using the source's 241 kcal/serving card, our slightly bigger thigh portion lands ~290–310 kcal with sauce). Tomatillo-pepper sauce ≈ 60 kcal/9C. Total ≈ 305 kcal / 34P / 10C / 11F. + rice 115 + lentil side 150 ≈ 570 kcal, ~38P.

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes 10 portions, 1.25×)
**Ingredients:**
- 1.6kg boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size chunks
- 1.25 lb (570g) tomatillos, husked + rinsed (~12–13 medium)
- 4 jalapeños, stemmed
- 2–3 poblano peppers, stemmed
- 1–2 serrano peppers (omit for milder — Kaz likes heat; keep 1)
- 2–3 white onions, quartered
- 3 cloves garlic
- 2.5 tbsp olive oil
- 2¼ tsp kosher salt (reduced from 1 tbsp)
- 1¼ cups water
- 2.5 tbsp lime juice (~1¼ limes)
- ⅓ cup chopped cilantro

**Method:**
1. Broil tomatillos + peppers on a foil-lined sheet, high, 5 min per side until blackened; steam covered 5 min; peel loose pepper skins.
2. Blend broiled veg + onions + garlic until smooth.
3. Sear the chicken in oil over medium-high ~15–20 min until browned; season with salt.
4. Pour in the green sauce + water; scrape the bottom; cover; simmer **30 min**.
5. Off heat: lime juice + cilantro; taste for salt.

**Storage:** Fridge 5–6 days; **freezer 3–4 months** (source-confirmed). Reheat microwave/stovetop.

#### Scaling Notes
- Scaled 1.25× (8 → 10 portions).
- **Salt reduced ~30%** (1 tbsp → 2¼ tsp) — the sear + broiled chiles carry the flavour.
- Naturally GF — no thickeners, no soy. Chicken thighs are the cheapest cut; tomatillos + poblanos are in season and local.
- Slow-cooker/Instant-Pot variants exist in the source but Kaz's kitchen is stovetop/oven.

### 🦐 DISH 3: Camarones a la Diabla
**Source:** https://inmamamaggieskitchen.com/camarones-a-la-diabla-mexican-deviled-shrimp/ (4.99★/81 votes)  
**Cross-check:** https://www.chilipeppermadness.com/recipes/camarones-a-la-diabla/ (same guajillo + árbol architecture)  
**Why this recipe:** Maggie Unzueta has published Mexican recipes since 2010; this is 4.99★ from 81 votes, 25 minutes start-to-finish, and the **sauce itself freezes up to 6 months** (source tip) — so the sauce is made mid-week and the shrimp are cooked fresh Friday. Shrimp never overcooks if you watch the pink. Naturally GF.

**Original servings:** 4 | **Scaled to:** 6 portions (1.5×), 4 assigned + 2 buffer

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown
| Nutrient | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Calories | ~300 kcal |
| Protein | 33g (52%) |
| Carbs | 12g (11%) |
| Fat | 12g (43%) |
| Fibre | 3g |

*Source card: 214 kcal/25P/9C/9F per 4-oz-shrimp serving — our bigger ~170g shrimp portion ≈ 300 kcal. Plate with arroz rojo (~115) + a side ≈ ~560.*

#### Macro Calculation Summary (USDA)
Shrimp 170g ≈ 170 kcal / 36P / 2F (USDA); diabla sauce per portion ≈ 80–100 kcal (guajillo + tomato + chipotle + 1 tbsp oil); total ≈ 330–350 kcal / 34P / 10C / 13F. With rice + side: ~570–600 kcal / ~35P / ~55C / ~16F — the leanest, most protein-dense plate. **Salt reduced ~70%** (source uses 1 tbsp for 4 servings → 2,641 mg sodium/card).

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes 6 portions, 1.5×)
**Ingredients:**
- 1.5 lb (~680g) large shrimp, peeled + deveined
- 6 guajillo chiles, stemmed + seeded
- 4–5 chiles de árbol (heat — use 2 for milder)
- 2 cups water (to rehydrate)
- 4–5 tomatoes, halved
- 1 chipotle pepper (+ 1 tsp adobo sauce)
- ¾ onion, diced (½ in the sauce, ¼ to the pan)
- 2 garlic cloves
- 3 tbsp olive oil (divided)
- 1½ tsp salt (reduced from 1 tbsp — the source's 2,641 mg/serving is a salt bomb)
- ¾ tsp black pepper

**Method:**
1. Stem + seed the guajillos and árboles. Cover with ~2 cups water, boil, off heat 5 min until pliable; discard the water.
2. Blend: softened chiles + tomatoes + chipotle + ¼ onion + garlic + salt. Strain. Set aside. *(Sauce keeps 5 days in the fridge / 6 months frozen — make it Wednesday or Thursday.)*
3. Pat the shrimp dry; season with salt + pepper.
4. Heat 1.5 tbsp oil in a large skillet; sauté the onion 1 min; add shrimp and cook **2 min** stirring, until just pink (do NOT overcook).
5. Lower the heat, add the sauce, stir 2–3 min. Taste for salt. Serve over arroz rojo with lime.

**Storage:** Fridge 3 days (shrimp is a fresh-Friday dish; the SAUCE freezes 6 months — make extra sauce, freeze it for future weeks). Reheat gently 5 min stovetop.

#### Scaling Notes
- Scaled 1.5× (source serves 4 with 1 lb shrimp → 6 portions at ~170g each; still a light, lean dish).
- **Salt reduced ~70%** (1 tbsp → 1.5 tsp for 6 portions) — the source's card is extremely salty.
- **Heat control:** 1 árbol = mild, 4–5 = diabla-level. Kaz likes bold — keep 4–5, add an extra tomato if it runs hot.
- The sauce is freezer-friendly (6 months, source tip) — make a double batch Wednesday and freeze half for a future week.

### 🥔 DISH 6a: Papas con Chorizo
**Source:** https://www.muydelish.com/chorizo-papas/ (5★/28 votes — Ana Frias, Sonora-born)  
**Cross-check:** https://www.chilipeppermadness.com/recipes/papas-con-chorizo/ + Allrecipes variant (quantities corroborate)  
**Why this recipe:** One pan, 30 minutes, dirt cheap (potatoes + a tube of chorizo), 28g protein per serving, fridge 1 week / freezer 3 months. The comment thread flags the chorizo grease — drain it (documented below).

**Original servings:** 4 | **Scaled to:** 12 side portions (3×), 8–10 assigned + buffer

| Nutrient | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Calories | ~165 kcal |
| Protein | 14g |
| Carbs | 15g |
| Fat | 6g |
| Fibre | 2g |

#### Macro Calculation Summary
Source card: 331 kcal / 30C / 28P / 12F per 1-cup serving. As a side (~⅓ cup) ≈ 165 kcal / 15C / 14P / 6F. USDA check: 60g potato ≈ 46 kcal; 28g chorizo ≈ 110 kcal/10F/6P; onion + oil ≈ 30 kcal → ~160–170 kcal per small side ✓.

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes ~12 side portions, 3×)
**Ingredients:**
- 2.25kg russet potatoes (~5 large), cut into 1.5cm cubes
- ~⅔ cup oil for frying (reduced — see note)
- 1.35kg Mexican chorizo (3 × 450g tubes)
- 3 cups diced white onion (~1½ large)
- Salt + pepper to taste
- *Optional garnish:* diced avocado, queso fresco, salsa, pickled jalapeños

**Method:**
1. Fry the potato cubes in oil (single layer, medium-high) ~10 min, stirring often, until golden; drain on paper towels. *(Air-fryer option: 200°C 15–20 min, shake halfway — less oil, Ana's own preferred method.)*
2. Same skillet, medium-high: cook the chorizo ~5 min, breaking it up; **drain excess grease** (comment-thread fix — store chorizo releases a lot). Add onion; cook 1–2 min until translucent.
3. Fold the potatoes back in; combine. Season.

**Storage:** Fridge 1 week; freezer 3 months. Reheat skillet 5–7 min or microwave.

#### Scaling Notes
- Scaled 3× (source serves 4 → 12 side portions).
- **Grease:** the author's own comment thread flags excess grease with store chorizo — **drain it** after frying the chorizo (paper-towel blot, keep ~1 tbsp for flavour). Kaz's lighter-hand preference applies.
- Frying 2.25kg of potatoes in 1½ cups of oil is excessive — use ~⅓ cup total, batched, or the author's air-fryer method (no air fryer assumed at Kaz's — use two skillets or fry in batches).

### 🫘 DISH 6b: Lentejas (Mexican Lentil Stew) — Oaxacan style
**Source:** https://food52.com/recipes/38323-lentejas-mexican-lentil-stew (Test Kitchen-Approved; learned from chef Oscar Carrizosa, Casa Crespo, Oaxaca)  
**Cross-check:** https://candidyadi.com/mexican-lentil-stew-lentejas-guisadas/ (freeze a month — source-confirmed) · allrecipes Lentils with Chorizo  
**Why this recipe:** Chef Oscar Carrizosa's Oaxacan lentil stew — dried lentils + bacon + plantain + pineapple + tomato. Sweet-savoury-smoky, unlike anything Kaz has had, dirt cheap (lentils + plantain + pineapple are market staples in Playa), and freezes well. Test Kitchen-approved on Food52.

**Original servings:** 4 as a meal | **Scaled to:** 12 side portions (2×), 8–10 assigned + buffer

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown (side portion ~½ cup)
| Nutrient | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Calories | ~160 kcal |
| Protein | 6g |
| Carbs | 24g |
| Fat | 4g |
| Fibre | 6g |

#### Macro Calculation Summary (USDA)
¼ cup dry green lentils ≈ 170 kcal / 12P / 30C; bacon ~1 slice-equivalent ≈ 45 kcal / 3.5F; plantain + pineapple ~40 kcal; oil ~2.5g ≈ 22 kcal → ~165 kcal / 8P / 26C / 4F per side portion. Lentils deliver ~11g protein + 8g fibre per side — the week's cheapest protein.

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes ~12 side portions, 1.5×)
**Ingredients:**
- 1.5 cups (300g) dried green lentils, rinsed
- 6 cups water
- 6 cloves garlic (2 smashed, 4 minced)
- 1½ white onions (¾ sliced, ¾ finely chopped)
- 1.5 tbsp vegetable oil
- 6 slices thick-cut bacon, cut into lardons
- ¾ tsp allspice
- 1½ ripe-ish plantains (green skin with black patches), peeled, sliced in rounds
- ~10 oz (280g) fresh pineapple, cored + chunked (¼ large pineapple)
- 3 plum tomatoes, seeded + chopped
- 1½ bunches cilantro — stems + ⅓ cup chopped leaves
- ¾ jalapeño
- 1–2 bay leaves
- Salt + pepper

**Method:**
1. Boil lentils + water with the smashed garlic + sliced onion 20 min until tender but intact; drain, **reserve the liquid**; season the lentils.
2. Same pot: oil + bacon lardons 5 min; add chopped onion 5 min; garlic + allspice 1 min.
3. Add plantain, pineapple, tomatoes; cook ~15 min until the plantain softens.
4. Return lentils + ~4.5 cups reserved liquid + cilantro stems + bay + jalapeño half; simmer 20 min until it thickens. Remove the aromatics; stir in cilantro leaves off-heat; season.

**Storage:** Fridge 5 days; freezer 1 month (source-confirmed — single-portion freezing works).

#### Scaling Notes
- Scaled 1.5× (1 cup → 1.5 cups lentils; the plantain + pineapple scale ~1.5×: 1.5 plantains, ~⅜ pineapple).
- Naturally GF (no thickeners). Bacon + lentils = cheap, hearty, freezer-friendly.
- The tropical twist (plantain + pineapple) is the recipe's identity — keep it; it's very Playa (both fruits are cheap and everywhere).

### 🦐 DISH 3: Camarones a la Diabla
**Source:** https://inmamamaggieskitchen.com/camarones-a-la-diabla-mexican-deviled-shrimp/ (4.99★/81 votes)

**Why this recipe:** 4.99★ from 81 votes, 25 minutes total, and the diabla sauce keeps 5 days refrigerated or 6 months frozen — perfect for the Fri best-fresh slot with a pre-made sauce. Naturally GF.

**Original servings:** 4 | **Scaled to:** 6 portions (1.5×), 4 assigned + 2 buffer

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown

|----------|--------|
| Calories | ~280 kcal |
| Protein | 33g (48%) |
| Carbs | 12g (15%) |
| Fat | 11g (47%) |

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes 6 portions, 1.5×)
**Ingredients:**
- 1.5 lb large shrimp (peeled + deveined)
- 6 guajillo chiles, stemmed + seeded
- 4–5 chiles de árbol (1–2 for milder), stemmed
- ½ chipotle pepper + 1 tsp adobo (source uses ½)
- 3 cups water
- 4–5 tomatoes, halved
- 1½ onions (¾ for the sauce, ¾ for the skillet)
- 1–2 garlic cloves
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1½ tsp salt + ¾ tsp black pepper

**Method:**
1. Stem + seed the chiles. Boil in ~3 cups water; off heat, soak 5 min until soft; **discard the water** (source: chiles can be dusty).
2. Blend: chiles + tomatoes + chipotle + ¼ onion + garlic + salt. **Strain** the sauce; set aside.
3. Season the shrimp (salt + pepper).
4. Heat 1.5 tbsp oil in a large skillet; sauté ¾ onion 1 min. Add the shrimp; stir 2 min until just pink. **Do not overcook.**
5. Low heat: add the sauce; simmer 2–3 min. Taste for salt. Serve over arroz rojo with lime.

**Storage:** The **sauce** freezes 6 months / fridge 5 days — make it ahead (Wed/Thu). Cooked shrimp: fridge 3 days max, **do not freeze** (rubbery — the source's own warning). This is a Fri/Sat fresh-only dish; the buffer portion is eaten as a bonus Saturday, not frozen.

#### Scaling Notes

- **Salt reduced ~70%** (1 tbsp → 1.5 tsp for the batch).
- **Heat:** guajillo (flavour) + árbol (heat) + ½ chipotle. Kaz wants bold — keep 4–5 arbols; milder = 1–2.
- Frozen shrimp is fine (thaw fully, pat dry).

### ⚠️ NOTES & CONSIDERATIONS

- **This is the cheapest week yet:** chuck + thighs + shrimp (small amount) + lentils + potatoes + rice. Rough basket: beef ~$260, thighs ~$200, shrimp ~$220, chorizo ~$120, produce ~$200, pantry ~$80 → **~MX$950–1,100 total** for 26+ plates. No specialty items — everything is in the normal Mexican supermarket.
- **GF:** every dish is naturally gluten-free (no flour, no soy, no beer anywhere). The only checks: bouillon labels and chorizo brands (plain Mexican chorizo is GF; avoid flavoured/honey varieties).
- **Shrimp freezes poorly cooked** (Mama Maggie's warning: rubbery) — shrimp portions are Fri/Sat fresh-only. Monday/Tuesday come from the beef + chicken braises, which freeze beautifully (both sources confirm 3 months).
- **Sodium watch:** the diabla source recipe uses 1 tbsp salt for 4 servings (2,641 mg sodium) — reduced ~70% in our batch. Taste at the end.
- **Chile colorado:** The Kitchn's test confirms **no flour is needed** — the sauce reduces on its own (GF win). Isabel's flour dusting is optional; if used, use GF (rice/tapioca).
- **Verde heat:** 3 jalapeños + 2 poblanos + 1 serrano is medium-bold; omit the serrano for milder (source tip). Kaz likes heat — keep as written, taste before serving.
- **Lime + cilantro finish** on both the verde and the diabla — don't skip (both authors call it essential).
- **Eat-by order:** shrimp first (Fri–Sat fresh, never frozen); beef + chicken hold 4–5 days and freeze great for Mon–Tue. Lentils improve on day 2–3.
- **Leftover old-batch portions** (pie v2, chopped BBQ, fish) should be finished Mon–Tue as planned — Wednesday lunch is the leftover slot if anything remains.
- **Continuity:** arroz rojo reuses the approved Bayless recipe (3rd repeat — rescaled to 2×). All five mains are brand-new dishes for Kaz.
