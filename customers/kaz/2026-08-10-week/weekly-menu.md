#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes 15 smaller portions; 11 assigned + 4 buffer)
**Ingredients:**
- 2 slabs pork loin spare/baby-back ribs, about 3.6kg / 8lb total
- 10 Tbsp light brown sugar
- 8 tsp smoked paprika
- 3 tsp black pepper
- 2 tsp garlic powder
- **4 tsp kosher salt total** (source would be 8 tsp; reduced as previously approved)
- 1 tsp chilli powder
- 1/2 tsp each onion powder, cumin, oregano, rubbed sage, marjoram, dried parsley, white pepper
- 2 cups white vinegar + 2 cups water

**Method:**
1. Heat oven to 121°C / 250°F. Mix sugar and spices. Reserve one-third.
2. Remove each membrane with a knife and paper towel. Pat ribs dry; coat both sides with two-thirds of rub.
3. Bake meat-side up on foil-lined trays about 3 hours, until tender.
4. Dip cooked racks briefly in vinegar-water, then apply reserved rub. Cut between bones and weigh portions consistently; divide both racks into 15 smaller portions. Pack 11 for meals and label 4 BUFFER.

**Storage:** Refrigerate seven assigned Wed–Sat portions up to 4 days. Friday, freeze four assigned Monday/Tuesday portions plus all four BUFFER portions; thaw assigned meals Sunday night or Monday night. Reheat covered at 150°C / 300°F.

### 🐟 DISH 2: Southern Oven-Fried Fish (Tilapia or Bagre)
**Source:** https://www.bowlofdelicious.com/four-ingredient-southern-style-oven-fried-catfish/  
**Cross-check:** https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/140180/southern-style-oven-fried-catfish/  
**Why this recipe:** Bowl of Delicious is rated 4.85/5 from 19 votes, is explicitly gluten-free, and states that tilapia, haddock, or cod can replace catfish. It also reports a successful reheated sandwich use, making it the best batch fish option.
**Original servings:** 4 | **Scaled to:** 12 portions (11 assigned + 1 buffer)

| Protein | 40g (43%) |
| Carbs | 24g (26%) |
| Fat | 13g (31%) |

#### Macro Calculation Summary
Nutrition database: [USDA FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/). Per tilapia portion: 170g raw tilapia ≈163 kcal/34P/3F; 1/2 egg ≈36 kcal/3P/0C/2.5F; 1/4 cup cornmeal (30.5g) ≈110 kcal/2.5P/23.5C/1F; tray oil counted conservatively at 7g ≈63 kcal/7F. Total ≈372 kcal/40P/24C/13F. Catfish has more fat and tracks the source card at about 411 kcal; both completed plates remain in range.

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes 12 portions; 11 assigned + 1 buffer)
**Ingredients:**
- 4.5lb / 2.04kg tilapia (mojarra) or bagre fillets, cut into 3–4 inch pieces
- 6 Tbsp olive oil for the trays
- 6 eggs, beaten
- 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 3/4 tsp black pepper
- 3 cups certified-GF cornmeal
- 3 Tbsp GF Cajun/Creole seasoning
- Lemon wedges and parsley, optional

**Method:**
1. Pat fish very dry. Heat oven to 246°C / 475°F. Oil two rimmed baking sheets.
2. Beat eggs with salt and pepper in one dish. Mix cornmeal and Creole seasoning in another. Dip every side of fish in egg, then press on the cornmeal coating.
3. Arrange without crowding. Bake 7 minutes, flip with a fish spatula, and bake 7 minutes more. Check thickest pieces are opaque and reach 63°C / 145°F.
4. Cool briefly on a rack; pack tartar separately. Do not seal steaming-hot fish or the crust will soften.

**Storage:** Best Wed–Sat. Friday, freeze four assigned portions plus the one BUFFER portion. Thaw assigned meals overnight. Reheat on a rack in a 220°C / 425°F oven or air fryer, not covered in the microwave. Tilapia is the practical Mexico substitute; cook by thickness, not name.

#### Scaling Notes
This is a linear 3× scale with one buffer. Only the fish identity changes if bagre is unavailable; the source explicitly allows white-fish substitutions. Use certified-GF cornmeal and GF Creole seasoning.

### 🥩 DISH 3: Chicken-Fried Steak with Gravy — fresh special
**Source:** https://feastandfarm.com/healthier-chicken-fried-steak/  
**Cross-check:** https://www.seriouseats.com/chicken-fried-steak-recipe  
**Why this recipe:** Feast and Farm publishes a GF cassava-flour option and tested shallow-frying/gravy instructions. It explicitly warns that the breading becomes soggy when held, so this is a two-portion Friday dinner special—not a freezer meal.
**Original servings:** 6 | **Scaled to:** 2 fresh portions

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown
| Nutrient | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Calories | 390 kcal |
| Protein | 32g (34%) |
| Carbs | 40g (42%) |
| Fat | 10g (24%) |
| Fibre | 1g |

#### Macro Calculation Summary
The source card reports 390 kcal/32P/40C/10F per steak with gravy. USDA beef, egg, milk, flour, and cornstarch values were checked against that result. Frying-oil absorption cannot be inferred from oil poured into the pan; the source’s tested serving value is retained. For an exact future measurement, weigh cooled frying fat before and after cooking.

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes 2 fresh portions)
**Ingredients:**
- 1/2lb / 227g cube steak, or 2 small round steaks
- **Meat mallet:** flat side for flattening/tenderizing
- 2/3 cup cassava flour or certified-GF 1:1 flour for coating
- 1/3 tsp salt, 1/6 tsp black pepper, 1/6 tsp garlic powder, pinch cayenne
- 2/3 beaten egg (beat 1 egg, weigh/use 34g, discard the rest)
- 2 Tbsp + 2 tsp / 40ml milk
- **3 cups / about 720ml lard or refined coconut oil** for approximately 1-inch frying depth in an 8-inch heavy skillet (most remains in the pan)
- 1 Tbsp pan drippings
- 1 1/2 tsp certified-GF cornstarch
- 2/3 cup milk; salt and pepper to taste

**Detailed meat preparation and mallet instructions:**
1. If buying round steak instead of cube steak, place each steak between two sheets of plastic wrap or a cut-open food bag. Use the **flat side of a meat mallet**—not the ridged tenderizer side—to flatten from the centre outward until each piece is an even 1/4-inch thick and about palm-sized.
2. Turn the steak over and repeat lightly. Do not pulverize it or make holes; the goal is even thickness and tenderness. Pat completely dry with paper towels.
3. Season the flour, not the raw meat, to avoid drawing moisture. Press steak into flour, dip in egg-milk, then press back into flour. Rest breaded steaks 5–7 minutes so the coating bonds.
4. Put 720ml frying fat in an 8-inch heavy skillet; it should be close to 1 inch deep. Heat to 185°C / 365–370°F. Fry one steak at a time, about 5 minutes first side and 4 minutes second side, until browned and beef reaches 63°C / 145°F, then rest at least 3 minutes. Drain on a wire rack.
5. For gravy, whisk cornstarch into cold milk. Heat 1 Tbsp drippings, add milk mixture, whisk constantly until simmering and thick, 4–5 minutes. Season and serve immediately over steak.

**Storage:** Do **not** make ahead, freeze, or refrigerate as a breaded meal. Cook Friday immediately before dinner and serve both portions at once. If a steak must be held briefly, keep it uncovered on a rack in a 93°C / 200°F oven; it will still lose crispness.

#### Scaling Notes
The food ingredients are one-third of the source batch. Frying fat is **not** reduced linearly because depth depends on skillet size, not number of steaks; the source’s 3-cup quantity is retained for an 8-inch skillet. This two-portion schedule respects the source’s explicit no-make-ahead warning.

### 🫘 DISH 5: Slow-Cooked Southern Green Beans
**Source:** https://www.stetted.com/southern-green-beans/  
**Cross-check:** https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/229960/southern-green-beans/  
**Why this recipe:** Stetted is rated 5/5 from 52 votes and publishes a bacon, onion, garlic, and broth method with no potatoes—keeping the side at 117 kcal and compatible with the calorie target. This is the approved green-bean choice.
**Original servings:** 8 | **Scaled to:** 15 portions (13 assigned + 2 buffer)

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown

|---|---:|
| Calories | 117 kcal |
| Protein | 5g (16%) |
| Carbs | 10g (33%) |
| Fat | 7g (51%) |

#### Macro Calculation Summary
Source card: 117 kcal/5P/10C/7F per serving. Scale 1.875× to 15 portions; per-serving values remain unchanged. Bacon and broth make sodium brand-dependent, so use unsalted broth and taste before salting.

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes 15 portions; 13 assigned + 2 buffer)
**Ingredients:**
- 3 3/4lb / 1.70kg fresh green beans, trimmed or cut into 2-inch pieces
- 12 strips bacon, chopped
- 1 7/8 cups chopped onion
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 3 3/4–5 5/8 cups labelled-GF unsalted chicken broth, enough to cover
- Salt and pepper only after tasting

**Method:**
1. Wash and trim beans. Cook bacon in a heavy pot until crisp; remove to paper towels.
2. Sauté onion in bacon fat until soft; add garlic 30 seconds.
3. Add beans and bacon. Pour in broth just to cover. Boil, reduce to low, cover, and simmer 1–2 hours, checking after 1 hour.
4. Season lightly, divide into 15 portions with a little potlikker; use 11 with ribs, 2 with CFS, and label 2 BUFFER.

**Storage:** Refrigerate 3–4 days; freeze the four assigned Mon–Tue portions and two BUFFER portions Friday. Nine assigned portions remain refrigerated for Wed–Sat (seven rib meals plus two CFS meals). Reheat gently with potlikker.

#### Scaling Notes
Linear 1.875× scale, rounded to practical kitchen quantities. The source says salt is to taste; bacon and broth supply salt, so no added salt is specified before tasting.

**Why this recipe:** This is the exact approved July 20 cornbread recipe, restored per Kaz’s instruction. Show Me the Yummy is rated 4.88/5 from 24 votes and reports a flourless, GF cornbread with cornmeal, creamed corn, and sweet corn.
**Original servings:** 8 | **Scaled to:** 2 pans, 32 small portions (24 used + 8 buffer)

#### Macro Calculation Summary
Source card reports 358 kcal/7P/47C/17F per 1/8 pan slice. Each pan is cut into 16 pieces, so each small slice is half: 179 kcal/3.5P/23.5C/8.5F. The Allrecipes GF creamed-corn cornbread independently confirms the cornmeal, creamed corn, egg, butter, milk, and sugar structure; the previously approved Show Me the Yummy formula remains unchanged.

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes 2 pans / 32 small portions)
**Ingredients, doubled exactly:**
- 1 cup unsalted butter, melted
- 1 1/3 cups granulated sugar
- 4 large eggs
- 2 cups buttermilk (or source’s milk-plus-vinegar substitute)
- 4 cups yellow cornmeal
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 cans (15oz each) GF-labelled cream-style corn
- 2 cans (15oz each) GF-labelled sweet corn, drained

**Method:**
1. Grease two cast-iron skillets or equivalent baking dishes. Heat oven to 190°C / 375°F.
2. Whisk melted butter, sugar, eggs, and buttermilk. Fold in cornmeal, baking soda, salt, creamed corn, and drained corn.
3. Divide between pans. Bake about 40 minutes, until fully cooked. Cool and cut each pan into 16 pieces.

**Storage:** Room temperature 1–2 days; fridge 5–7 days; freeze 2–3 months. Reheat by microwave or oven.

#### Scaling Notes
This is the unchanged previous recipe made twice. The only operational change is cutting each pan into 16 small meal-prep pieces instead of 8 standard pieces; no ingredient or method change.
