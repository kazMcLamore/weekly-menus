### 🍝 DISH 1: Ragù alla Bolognese (slowly simmered meat sauce)
**Source:** https://playswellwithbutter.com/bolognese-sauce-recipe/ (4.9★/63 reviews, GF-tagged, ten years in the making; author trained at a fine-dining Italian restaurant)  
**Cross-check:** https://simplehomeedit.com/recipe/slow-cooker-beef-ragu/ (chuck + passata, stovetop variant) · https://www.kitchensanctuary.com/big-batch-beef-ragu-with-pasta/ (big-batch, freezer-first design)  
**Why this recipe:** A signature restaurant bolognese (beef + pork + pancetta pestata, caramelized soffritto, red wine, 90-minute simmer, cream + parmesan finish) that's explicitly GF, serves 10–12 in one batch, keeps 4–5 days in the fridge and 3 months frozen — the ideal Wednesday long-braise. Wednesday's heavy simmer runs unattended while the cacciatore and peperonata cook alongside.

**Original servings:** 10–12 | **Scaled to:** 11 portions (1× recipe, portioned to 11), 9 assigned + 2 buffer  

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown (sauce only, before pasta)
| Nutrient | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Calories | ~300 kcal |
| Protein | 22g (29%) |
| Carbs | 14g (19%) |
| Fat | 18g (54%) |
| Fibre | 2g |

*Plate with ~150g GF pasta (~210 kcal) + peperonata (~150 kcal) ≈ ~660 kcal.*

#### Macro Calculation Summary (USDA)
Batch total ≈ ground beef 450g (800 kcal/85P/50F) + ground pork 450g (790/80P/54F) + bacon 115g (380/14P/36F) + soffritto veg (~150/3P/30C) + tomato paste 170g (140/8P/30C) + red wine 235ml (125) + crushed tomatoes 800g (260/11P/56C) + cream 120ml (340/3P/28F) + parm 45g (180/16P/12F) + oil 30g (270/30F) ≈ ~3,340 kcal / 213P / 120C / 210F. Per 11 portions ≈ ~300 kcal / 19P / 11C / 19F. Source verifies "serves 10–12"; 19P per portion is conservative (their card says ~20g+). With GF pasta + peperonata: ~650–680 kcal / ~27P / ~57C / ~24F.

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes 11 portions — 1× the source)
**Ingredients:**
- 2 large carrots + 1 stalk celery + 1 large yellow onion (soffritto, pulsed fine)
- 4 oz (115g) pancetta, diced (or thick-cut bacon — author-approved swap; Playa has no pancetta counter reliably)
- 6 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 450g (1 lb) ground beef
- 450g (1 lb) ground pork
- 1 can (6 oz / 170g) tomato paste
- 1 cup (240ml) medium-bodied dry red wine (Chianti/merlot — any drinkable dry red; NOT "cooking wine")
- 3–4 sprigs fresh rosemary + 10–12 sprigs fresh thyme (tied in a bundle) + 2 bay leaves
- Optional: 1 parmesan rind
- 1 can (28 oz / 800g) crushed tomatoes
- up to 2 cups chicken broth or water
- kosher salt + black pepper
- *Finish:* ½ cup heavy cream + ½ cup grated parmesan
- *Serve:* GF pasta (below) + parmesan + parsley/basil + pinch of red pepper flakes

**Method:**
1. Pulse the carrots/celery/onion to fine pieces (soffritto); pulse the pancetta + garlic to a paste (pestata).
2. Render the pestata in the olive oil 4–5 min; add the soffritto + 1 tsp salt; brown deeply 15–20 min.
3. Season the meats; add to the pot, sear 2–3 min undisturbed, break up finely; stir in the tomato paste and cook 3–4 min (cook off the raw taste).
4. Deglaze with the wine, scraping the fond; simmer 4–5 min until nearly evaporated.
5. Add the herb bundle, bay, parm rind, crushed tomatoes + ~1 cup broth/water. Partially cover and **simmer 90 min**, stirring occasionally, loosening with more broth/water as it reduces.
6. Remove the herbs/bay/rind; taste and season. *Cool and portion here for batch use.*
7. *Per meal:* reheat a portion of sauce with a splash of cream/parm; toss the reheated GF pasta through it 1–2 min with a splash of pasta water.

**Storage:** Fridge 4–5 days; **freezer 3 months** (source-confirmed; freezes before or after the pasta is added). Reheat with a splash of water.

#### Scaling Notes
- 1× recipe portioned into 11 (the source's own 10–12 range) — no scaling adjustments needed. Batch-size bonus: the sauce is designed for big-batch freezing.
- **GF:** the recipe is already gluten-free as written (no flour anywhere); serve with GF pasta.
- **Pancetta → bacon swap** is the author's own recommended substitution when pancetta isn't available (Playa supermarkets rarely stock pancetta; bacon is cheap and everywhere).
- **Salt:** season at each browning stage to taste (~1 tsp soffritto + 1 tsp meat), no fixed total — keep a light hand per Kaz's preference.

### 🍗 DISH 2: Pollo alla Cacciatora (Hunter-Style Chicken)
**Source:** https://www.onceuponachef.com/recipes/chicken-cacciatore.html (4.88★/81 reviews, chef-tested & perfected; stovetop braise)  
**Cross-check:** https://www.themediterraneandish.com/chicken-cacciatore-recipe/ (same thigh + crushed-tomato + wine architecture)  
**Why this recipe:** Jenn Segal's tested version — bone-in thighs braised in a rich tomato–red-wine sauce with mushrooms, peppers, sage and rosemary until fall-apart tender; make-ahead and freezer-friendly (3 months, source-confirmed). GF-adaptable with one swap (flour dredge → cornstarch — a standard GF adaptation, and the site tags it gluten-free-adaptable).

**Original servings:** 4 (8 thighs) | **Scaled to:** 10 portions (2.5× → 20 thighs), 9 assigned + 1 buffer  

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown
| Nutrient | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Calories | ~720 kcal |
| Protein | 52g (29%) |
| Carbs | 28g (16%) |
| Fat | 24g (30%) |
| Fibre | 4g |

*Source card: 790 kcal / 88P / 42C / 26F per 2-thigh serving (with flour dredge). Our portion = 1 thigh + sauce + GF pasta (~210) + trifolate (~110) ≈ ~700–730 kcal.*

#### Macro Calculation Summary (USDA)
1 bone-in thigh (~150g cooked meat ≈ 290 kcal/34P/17F) + sauce share (broth/tomatoes/wine/veg ≈ 200 kcal/12C) + GF pasta 150g (~210/4P/44C) + trifolate side (~110) ≈ ~720 kcal / ~48P / ~62C / ~25F. Slightly rich — the flour dredge is dropped (GF), and the sauce uses honey sparingly (1.25 tbsp per batch).

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes 10 portions, 2.5×)
**Ingredients:**
- 20 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (~4.5kg total), trimmed of excess skin
- 5 tsp kosher salt, divided (reduced ~20% from 6.25 tsp equivalent)
- 1¼ tsp black pepper
- 1¼ cups **cornstarch** (for the dredge — replaces the source's ½ cup × 2.5 all-purpose flour; a light dusting is all that's needed)
- 5 tbsp olive oil
- 2½ large yellow onions, halved + thinly sliced
- 15 cloves garlic, minced
- 500g (1 lb) bella/cremini mushrooms, sliced
- 5 red bell peppers, cut into ¼-in strips
- 1¾ cups + 2 tbsp dry red wine
- 5 cups chicken broth (GF)
- 2½ cans (14.5 oz each) fire-roasted or regular diced tomatoes
- ⅝ cup tomato paste
- 5 tbsp chopped fresh sage
- 2½ tbsp chopped fresh rosemary
- 2½ tsp dried oregano
- 2½ tbsp honey
- Fresh parsley, for garnish

**Method:**
1. Blot the thighs; season with 3¾ tsp salt + pepper; dust lightly with cornstarch.
2. Brown in the oil, skin-side down, 5–7 min per batch until golden; set aside. Pour off all but 2½ tbsp fat.
3. Sauté the onions (5 min) + garlic (1 min, don't brown) + mushrooms + peppers (5 min).
4. Add the wine; boil 5–6 min until mostly evaporated, scraping the fond.
5. Add broth, tomatoes, tomato paste, sage, rosemary, oregano, honey + remaining 1¼ tsp salt; simmer uncovered 10 min.
6. Return the chicken + juices; cover; simmer medium-low **35 min** (to 74°C / 165°F internal).
7. Transfer the chicken, discard the skin (source's technique — it goes soggy), reduce the sauce a few minutes, return the chicken, spoon sauce over, garnish with parsley.

**Storage:** Fridge 3 days (make-ahead confirmed); **freezer 3 months**. Thaw overnight; reheat stovetop medium-low.

#### Scaling Notes
- Scaled 2.5× (8 → 20 thighs) for 10 portions (2 thighs → 1 portion halves the source's very hearty serving — the source card at 2 thighs/serving is 790 kcal; at 1 thigh + sauce + pasta + side the plate lands ~720).
- **GF dredge:** cornstarch replaces the all-purpose flour (¼ of the quantity — cornstarch browns faster; dust lightly).
- **Salt reduced ~20%** (6.25 tsp → 5 tsp across 10 portions).
- **Honey:** kept (1 tbsp per 4 servings — balances the tomato); it's the source's signature touch.

### 🍤 DISH 3: Shrimp Pomodoro (Gamberi al Pomodoro)
**Source:** https://www.kimscravings.com/pasta-pomodoro/ (4.96★/23 reviews — 7 ingredients, 30 minutes)  
**Cross-check:** https://www.simplyrecipes.com/recipes/pasta_pomodoro_with_shrimp/ (same shrimp → garlic → fresh-tomato → basil sequence) · https://www.southernliving.com/recipes/shrimp-and-burst-cherry-tomato-pasta (burst-tomato variant)  
**Why this recipe:** The classic pomodoro — fresh tomatoes, garlic, basil, EVOO — with lean shrimp. 7 ingredients, 30 minutes, and the sauce alone **freezes for 3 months** (source tip), so a double sauce batch gets banked. Naturally GF with the GF pasta. Friday's best-fresh dish.

**Original servings:** 4 | **Scaled to:** 6 portions (1.5×), 4 assigned + 2 buffer  

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown
| Nutrient | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Calories | ~640 kcal |
| Protein | 44g (28%) |
| Carbs | 60g (38%) |
| Fat | 17g (24%) |
| Fibre | 5g |

*Source card: 580 kcal / 69C / 40P / 15F per serving (incl. pasta). Our GF-pasta plate with a veg side ≈ ~640 kcal / ~44P / ~60C / ~17F.*

#### Macro Calculation Summary (USDA)
Shrimp 170g ≈ 180 kcal / 38P / 2F; pomodoro sauce (3 large tomatoes + garlic + EVOO ≈ 120 kcal / 12C / 9F); GF pasta ~150g cooked ≈ 210 kcal / 44C / 4P; trifolate side ≈ 110 kcal; parm ~10g ≈ 40 kcal. Total ≈ 640 kcal / ~50P / ~60C / ~18F (P 31% / C 37% / F 25%).

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes 6 portions, 1.5×)
**Ingredients:**
- 1.5 lb (~680g) shrimp, peeled + deveined
- 3 tbsp + 1.5 tsp olive oil (divided)
- 6–8 large ripe tomatoes, chopped (or 2 cups cherry tomatoes, halved — burst-tomato variant)
- 4–5 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 3 tbsp chopped fresh basil, + more to garnish
- ⅓ tsp crushed red pepper flakes (optional heat — Kaz likes bold, keep it)
- GF pasta (below)
- ¾ cup freshly grated parmesan (check label — buy a block and grate; pre-grated has anti-caking cellulose)
- salt + pepper
- Splash of reserved pasta water to loosen

**Method:**
1. Boil water for the pasta (fresh pot per meal, or use Wednesday's batch).
2. Sauté the shrimp in 1.5 tbsp oil 1–2 min per side until pink; remove. **Don't clean the pan** — the browned bits deepen the sauce (source tip).
3. Same skillet, medium-low: 1.5 tbsp oil + tomatoes + salt/pepper; 5 min; add garlic; 2–3 min until soft and fragrant.
4. Return the shrimp + basil + a drizzle of oil; combine. Red pepper flakes for heat.
5. Toss with the GF pasta + a splash of pasta water; top with parmesan + basil.

**Storage:** Fridge 4–5 days (source); the **sauce freezes 3 months on its own** — make a double sauce batch and bank half. Cooked shrimp: Fri/Sat fresh only (rubbery when frozen — same warning as the camarones week).

#### Scaling Notes
- Scaled 1.5× (1 lb → 1.5 lb shrimp; 4 → 6 portions). Shrimp is the priciest protein — 6 portions keeps the basket lean-cheap; the plate leans on pasta + sides for bulk.
- **Naturally GF** with the GF pasta; the dish has no flour anywhere.
- Salt: season to taste (the source uses salt-to-taste; parm adds saltiness too).

### 🥬 DISH 4: Peperonata (Italian Stewed Sweet Peppers)
**Source:** https://www.themediterraneandish.com/peperonata-recipe/ (by Domenica Marchetti — author of 8 Italian cookbooks; reviewed/founder Suzy Karadsheh, NYT bestselling)  
**Cross-check:** https://www.savoringitaly.com/peperonata/ (Calabrian nonna version — same peppers/onion/EVOO architecture) · https://www.seriouseats.com/peperonata-bell-pepper-italian-recipe (technique: garlic + onion first, plenty of olive oil)  
**Why this recipe:** Silky sweet peppers stewed low in olive oil with onions, tomato sauce and capers — a *cucina povera* classic that keeps 5 days in the fridge and **improves as it sits** (source-confirmed). Sweet contrast to the two rich red-sauce meats. Naturally GF.

**Original servings:** 6 | **Scaled to:** 11 portions (1.8×), 9 assigned + 2 buffer  

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown
| Nutrient | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Calories | ~150 kcal |
| Protein | 3g |
| Carbs | 16g |
| Fat | 10g |
| Fibre | 5g |

*Source card: 150 kcal / 16C / 3P / 10F per serving.*

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes ~11 portions, 1.8×)
**Ingredients:**
- 7 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil (~110ml)
- 1¾ medium yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 1–1½ fennel bulbs, quartered + thinly sliced (optional — the author's signature addition; omit for cost)
- 3.6 lb (~1.6kg) sweet ripe bell peppers (red or red/yellow/orange mix — NOT green, too bitter), thinly sliced
- 3–4 garlic cloves, lightly crushed
- kosher salt
- 1¾ cups tomato sauce (blend crushed tomatoes, or use passata)
- 1¾ tbsp capers, rinsed + patted dry
- 3½ tbsp balsamic vinegar (or white balsamic)
- ⅜ cup torn fresh basil + mint

**Method:**
1. Cold pan: EVOO + onion + fennel + peppers + garlic; heat to medium; sauté 10 min, tossing.
2. Season (1¾ tsp salt); partially cover; 10–15 min until softened but with a little bite.
3. Add the tomato sauce + capers; cook uncovered ~10 min until reduced and silky.
4. Medium-high: add the balsamic; 1 min lively simmer, then 2–3 min low. Off heat: basil + mint. Rest 10 min; discard the garlic cloves; drizzle with EVOO.

**Storage:** Fridge 5 days — **better on day 2–3** (source). Serve warm, room temp or cold. Freezes acceptably (softer peppers on thaw).

#### Scaling Notes
- Scaled 1.8× (6 → ~11 side portions).
- **Fennel is the author's optional addition** — omit it for cost/simplicity (documented; it's not traditional anyway).
- Naturally GF; vegan side; no thickeners.

### 🥒 DISH 5: Zucchine Trifolate (Italian Sautéed Zucchini)
**Source:** https://www.lacucinaitaliana.com/italian-food/how-to-cook/love-zucchini-cook (La Cucina Italiana — the authority, since 1929)  
**Cross-check:** https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/zucchine-trifolati-2106967 (onion version, 8–10 min) · https://italianfoodforever.com/2018/07/zucchini-trifolati/ (garlic + parsley version, low heat)  
**Why this recipe:** The simplest Italian side: zucchini + EVOO + garlic + parsley (or onion), sautéed hot-and-fast then finished gently. No liquid, no thickeners — naturally GF. Trifolati is a *method*, so the batch quantities below are the two sources' consensus.

**Original servings:** 4 | **Scaled to:** 11 portions (2.75×), 9 assigned + 2 buffer  

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown
| Nutrient | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Calories | ~110 kcal |
| Protein | 2g |
| Carbs | 8g |
| Fat | 8g |
| Fibre | 2.5g |

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes ~11 portions, 2.75×)
**Ingredients:**
- 2.75 kg zucchini (~6 medium-large), quartered lengthwise + cut into thick half-moons (seeds removed if watery — La Cucina's tip; save seeds for soup)
- 7 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 large onion, finely chopped (La Cucina prefers onion + garlic) + 6 garlic cloves, slivered or minced
- 1 bunch fresh parsley, chopped (or basil/mint in season)
- kosher salt + black pepper
- *Optional (PBS variant):* 3–4 tomatoes, seeded + chopped, with the basil — a wetter, saucier version

**Method:**
1. Sauté the onion in the EVOO over medium-high ~1–2 min (Food Network timing); add the zucchini in a single layer (work in batches — do not crowd); sauté until golden and just soft, 8–10 min, stirring.
2. Add the garlic for the last 1–2 min (don't brown it).
3. Off heat: salt, pepper + a big shower of parsley. Serve warm or room temp. **No water added** — the zucchini releases its own (La Cucina's explicit instruction).

**Storage:** Fridge 4 days. Freezes acceptably (softens — fine for Mon–Tue). Reheat quickly in a skillet or microwave.

#### Scaling Notes
- Scaled 2.75× on the zucchini (a method dish — quantities from the two cross-check sources' consensus: enough EVOO to coat, garlic + parsley to taste).
- Sauté in batches so the zucchini browns instead of steaming.
- Naturally GF; vegan.

### ⚠️ NOTES & CONSIDERATIONS

- **GF handling this week:** every sauce is naturally gluten-free; the two watch-points are the **GF pasta product** (Barilla GF — check the label + shelf at Walmart; polenta fallback from pantry cornmeal if out of stock) and the **cacciatore dredge** (cornstarch replaces the flour — ¼ the quantity, dust lightly).
- **Parmesan:** buy a block and grate fresh — pre-grated parm often contains cellulose anti-caking (label-check if buying pre-grated).
- **Shrimp:** Fri/Sat fresh-only (rubbery when frozen — same warning as the camarones week). The pomodoro **sauce** freezes 3 months — double it Wednesday and bank half.
- **GF pasta freeze caveat:** Mon–Tue pasta is softer after the short freeze; sauce absorbs into it and it's fine — fallback is a fresh 10-minute pot on Monday.
- **Portion reality:** the cacciatore source serves 2 thighs per person (790 kcal/serving!); our plate = 1 thigh + sauce + pasta + side ≈ ~720 kcal — still the week's richest plate. The bolognese and shrimp plates are leaner; the week averages ~680.
- **Eat-by order:** shrimp first (Fri–Sat fresh); bolognese + cacciatore hold 4–5 days and freeze well for Mon–Tue; peperonata improves on day 2–3 (source).
- **Wednesday is the heavy day (~5 hr):** two braises + peperonata + pasta batch; Thursday is nearly free (trifolate = 15 min); Friday is 30 min + routing.
- **Continuity:** all six dishes are new to Kaz's rotation (first Italian week — verified against the full dish history).
- **Budget:** beef + pork blend, bacon (not pancetta), thighs, 1.5 lb shrimp, GF pasta (3 boxes ~MX$130) — basket lands ~MX$1,000–1,200; the GF pasta is the week's only premium item.
