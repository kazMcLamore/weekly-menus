### 🍝 DISH 1: Ragù alla Bolognese (slowly simmered meat sauce)
**Source:** https://playswellwithbutter.com/bolognese-sauce-recipe/ (4.9★/63 reviews, GF-tagged, ten years in the making; author trained at a fine-dining Italian restaurant)  
**Cross-check:** https://simplehomeedit.com/recipe/slow-cooker-beef-ragu/ (5★/33; soffritto + tomato paste + wine + passata, with stovetop and freezer instructions)
**Why this recipe:** A signature restaurant bolognese (beef + pork + pancetta pestata, caramelized soffritto, red wine, 90-minute simmer, cream + parmesan finish) that's explicitly GF, serves 10–12 in one batch, keeps 4–5 days in the fridge and 3 months frozen — the ideal Wednesday long-braise. Wednesday's heavy simmer runs unattended while the cacciatore and peperonata cook alongside.

**Original servings:** 10–12 | **Scaled to:** 11 portions (1× recipe, portioned to 11), 9 assigned + 2 buffer  

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown (full plate)
| Nutrient | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Calories | ~670 kcal |
| Protein | 26g (16%) |
| Carbs | 74g (44%) |
| Fat | 30g (40%) |
| Fibre | 7g |

*Full plate = Bolognese sauce + ~150g GF pasta + peperonata.*

#### Macro Calculation Summary (USDA)
Batch sauce total ≈ ground beef 450g (800 kcal/85P/50F) + ground pork 450g (790/80P/54F) + bacon 115g (380/14P/36F) + soffritto veg (~150/3P/30C) + tomato paste 170g (140/8P/30C) + red wine 235ml (125) + crushed tomatoes 800g (260/11P/56C) + cream 120ml (340/3P/28F) + parm 45g (180/16P/12F) + oil 30g (270/30F) ≈ ~3,340 kcal / 213P / 120C / 210F. Per 11 sauce portions ≈ 304 kcal / 19P / 11C / 19F. Add GF pasta (~214 kcal / 4P / 47C / 1F) and peperonata (150 kcal / 3P / 16C / 10F) → **~670 kcal / 26P / 74C / 30F**.

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes 11 portions — 1× the source)
**Ingredients:**
- 2 large carrots + 1 stalk celery + 1 large yellow onion (soffritto, pulsed fine)
- 4 oz (115g) pancetta, diced (or thick-cut bacon — author-approved swap; Playa has no pancetta counter reliably)
- 6 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 450g (1 lb) ground beef
- 450g (1 lb) ground pork
- 1 can (6 oz / 170g) tomato paste
- 1 cup (240ml) medium-bodied dry red wine (Chianti/merlot — any drinkable dry red; NOT "cooking wine")
- 3–4 sprigs fresh rosemary + 10–12 sprigs fresh thyme (tied in a bundle) + 2 bay leaves
- Optional: 1 parmesan rind
- 1 can (28 oz / 800g) crushed tomatoes
- up to 2 cups chicken broth or water
- kosher salt + black pepper
- *Finish:* ½ cup heavy cream + ½ cup grated parmesan
- *Serve:* GF pasta (below) + parmesan + parsley/basil + pinch of red pepper flakes

**Method:**
1. Pulse the carrots/celery/onion to fine pieces (soffritto); pulse the pancetta + garlic to a paste (pestata).
2. Render the pestata in the olive oil 4–5 min; add the soffritto + 1 tsp salt; brown deeply 15–20 min.
3. Season the meats; add to the pot, sear 2–3 min undisturbed, break up finely; stir in the tomato paste and cook 3–4 min (cook off the raw taste).
4. Deglaze with the wine, scraping the fond; simmer 4–5 min until nearly evaporated.
5. Add the herb bundle, bay, parm rind, crushed tomatoes + ~1 cup broth/water. Partially cover and **simmer 90 min**, stirring occasionally, loosening with more broth/water as it reduces.
6. Remove the herbs/bay/rind; taste and season. *Cool and portion here for batch use.*
7. *Per meal:* reheat a portion of sauce with a splash of cream/parm; toss the reheated GF pasta through it 1–2 min with a splash of pasta water.

**Storage:** Fridge 4–5 days; **freezer 3 months** (source-confirmed; freezes before or after the pasta is added). Reheat with a splash of water.

#### Scaling Notes
- 1× recipe portioned into 11 (the source's own 10–12 range) — no scaling adjustments needed. Batch-size bonus: the sauce is designed for big-batch freezing.
- **GF:** the recipe is already gluten-free as written (no flour anywhere); serve with GF pasta.
- **Pancetta → bacon swap** is the author's own recommended substitution when pancetta isn't available (Playa supermarkets rarely stock pancetta; bacon is cheap and everywhere).
- **Salt:** season at each browning stage to taste (~1 tsp soffritto + 1 tsp meat), no fixed total — keep a light hand per Kaz's preference.

### 🍗 DISH 2: Pollo alla Cacciatora (Hunter-Style Chicken)
**Source:** https://www.onceuponachef.com/recipes/chicken-cacciatore.html (4.88★/81 reviews, chef-tested & perfected; stovetop braise)  
**Cross-check:** https://www.themediterraneandish.com/chicken-cacciatore-recipe/ (same thigh + crushed-tomato + wine architecture)  
**Why this recipe:** Jenn Segal's tested version — bone-in thighs braised in a rich tomato–red-wine sauce with mushrooms, peppers, sage and rosemary until fall-apart tender; make-ahead and freezer-friendly (3 months, source-confirmed). GF-adaptable with one swap (flour dredge → cornstarch — a standard GF adaptation, and the site tags it gluten-free-adaptable).

**Original servings:** 4 (8 thighs; 2 thighs per source serving) | **Scaled to:** 10 one-thigh portions (1.25× formula → 10 thighs), 9 assigned + 1 buffer

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown (full plate)
| Nutrient | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Calories | ~690 kcal |
| Protein | 50g (29%) |
| Carbs | 70g (41%) |
| Fat | 22g (29%) |
| Fibre | 6g |

*Source card: 790 kcal / 88P / 42C / 26F per 2-thigh serving. Our portion is 1 thigh + half a source serving of sauce, GF pasta and trifolate.*

#### Macro Calculation Summary (USDA)
Half of the source's cacciatore serving is ~395 kcal / 44P / 21C / 13F. Replacing its flour with a light cornstarch dust lowers this to about 375 kcal / 44P / 15C / 13F. Add GF pasta (~214 / 4P / 47C / 1F) + trifolate (~110 / 2P / 8C / 8F) → **~690 kcal / 50P / 70C / 22F**.

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes 10 one-thigh portions, 1.25×)
**Ingredients:**
- 10 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs (~2.25kg total), trimmed of excess skin
- 2½ tsp kosher salt, divided (reduced ~20% from the 3⅛ tsp linear scale)
- ⅝ tsp black pepper
- 3 tbsp **cornstarch** (a light dredge — replaces the ⅝ cup flour at this scale)
- 2½ tbsp olive oil
- 1¼ large yellow onions, halved + thinly sliced
- 8 cloves garlic, minced
- 285g (10 oz) bella/cremini mushrooms, sliced
- 3 red bell peppers, cut into ¼-in strips
- 1 cup dry red wine
- 2½ cups chicken broth (GF)
- 1¼ cans (14.5 oz each) fire-roasted or regular diced tomatoes (buy 2 cans; reserve the remainder)
- 5 tbsp tomato paste
- 2½ tbsp chopped fresh sage
- 1¼ tbsp chopped fresh rosemary
- 1¼ tsp dried oregano
- 1¼ tbsp honey
- Fresh parsley, for garnish

**Method:**
1. Blot the thighs; season with 2 tsp salt + pepper; dust lightly with cornstarch.
2. Brown in the oil, skin-side down, 5–7 min per batch until golden; set aside. Pour off all but 2½ tbsp fat.
3. Sauté the onions (5 min) + garlic (1 min, don't brown) + mushrooms + peppers (5 min).
4. Add the wine; boil 5–6 min until mostly evaporated, scraping the fond.
5. Add broth, tomatoes, tomato paste, sage, rosemary, oregano, honey + remaining ½ tsp salt; simmer uncovered 10 min.
6. Return the chicken + juices; cover; simmer medium-low **35 min** (to 74°C / 165°F internal).
7. Transfer the chicken, discard the skin (source's technique — it goes soggy), reduce the sauce a few minutes, return the chicken, spoon sauce over, garnish with parsley.

**Storage:** Fridge 3 days (make-ahead confirmed); **freezer 3 months**. Thaw overnight; reheat stovetop medium-low.

#### Scaling Notes
- Scaled 1.25× on ingredients (8 → 10 thighs), then portioned at **1 thigh per plate** rather than the source's 2-thigh serving.
- **GF dredge:** 3 tbsp cornstarch replaces ⅝ cup all-purpose flour; cornstarch browns faster, so dust lightly.
- **Salt reduced ~20%** (3⅛ tsp linear → 2½ tsp across 10 portions).
- **Honey:** kept at the linear 1¼ tbsp — it balances the tomato.

### 🍤 DISH 3: Shrimp Pomodoro (Gamberi al Pomodoro)
**Source:** https://www.kimscravings.com/pasta-pomodoro/ (4.96★/23 reviews — 7 ingredients, 30 minutes)  
**Cross-check:** https://ciaoflorentina.com/tomato-basil-pasta-sauce/ (5★; same shrimp + fresh tomato + garlic + basil + pasta architecture)
**Why this recipe:** The classic pomodoro — fresh tomatoes, garlic, basil, EVOO — with lean shrimp. 7 ingredients, 30 minutes, and the sauce alone **freezes for 3 months** if intentionally doubled in a future batch. Naturally GF with the GF pasta. Friday's best-fresh dish.

**Original servings:** 4 | **Scaled to:** 6 larger portions (2.25× ingredients, divided into 6), 4 assigned + 2 weekend buffer

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown (full plate)
| Nutrient | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Calories | ~710 kcal |
| Protein | 50g (28%) |
| Carbs | 65g (37%) |
| Fat | 27g (34%) |
| Fibre | 5g |

*Source card: 580 kcal / 69C / 40P / 15F per standard serving (including pasta). Our portion has more shrimp and sauce, a moderate GF-pasta portion, and trifolate.*

#### Macro Calculation Summary (USDA)
Shrimp 170g ≈ 180 kcal / 38P / 2F; the larger pomodoro sauce share + parmesan ≈ 205 kcal / 10C / 16F / 6P; GF pasta ~150g cooked ≈ 214 kcal / 47C / 4P / 1F; trifolate ≈ 110 kcal / 2P / 8C / 8F. Total ≈ **710 kcal / 50P / 65C / 27F**.

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes 6 larger portions, 2.25×)
**Ingredients:**
- 2.25 lb (~1kg) shrimp, peeled + deveined
- 5 tbsp + 1 tsp olive oil (divided)
- 9–11 large ripe tomatoes, chopped (or 4½ cups cherry tomatoes, halved — burst-tomato variant)
- 7 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 4½ tbsp chopped fresh basil, + more to garnish
- ½ tsp crushed red pepper flakes (optional heat — Kaz likes bold, keep it)
- GF pasta (below)
- 1⅛ cups (~100g) freshly grated parmesan
- salt + pepper
- Splash of reserved pasta water to loosen

**Method:**
1. Boil water for the pasta (fresh pot per meal, or use Wednesday's batch).
2. Sauté the shrimp in 2 tbsp oil 1–2 min per side until pink; remove. **Don't clean the pan** — the browned bits deepen the sauce (source tip).
3. Same skillet, medium-low: 2 tbsp oil + tomatoes + salt/pepper; 5 min; add garlic; 2–3 min until soft and fragrant.
4. Return the shrimp + basil + the remaining oil as needed; combine. Red pepper flakes for heat.
5. Toss with the GF pasta + a splash of pasta water; top with parmesan + basil.

**Storage:** Fridge 4–5 days (source); the **sauce freezes 3 months on its own** if intentionally doubled in a future batch. Cooked shrimp: Fri/Sat fresh only (rubbery when frozen — same warning as the camarones week).

#### Scaling Notes
- Scaled 2.25×, then divided into 6 larger portions (~170g shrimp each) so the complete plate lands near the calorie target.
- **Naturally GF** with the GF pasta; the dish has no flour anywhere.
- Salt: season to taste (the source uses salt-to-taste; parm adds saltiness too).

### 🥬 DISH 4: Peperonata (Italian Stewed Sweet Peppers)
**Source:** https://www.themediterraneandish.com/peperonata-recipe/ (by Domenica Marchetti — author of 8 Italian cookbooks; reviewed/founder Suzy Karadsheh, NYT bestselling)  
**Cross-check:** https://www.savoringitaly.com/peperonata/ (5★; Calabrian nonna version — same peppers/onion/EVOO/passata architecture)
**Why this recipe:** Silky sweet peppers stewed low in olive oil with onions, tomato sauce and capers — a *cucina povera* classic that keeps 5 days in the fridge and **improves as it sits** (source-confirmed). Sweet contrast to the two rich red-sauce meats. Naturally GF.

**Original servings:** 6 | **Scaled to:** 13 portions (2.2×), 11 assigned + 2 buffer

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown
| Nutrient | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Calories | ~150 kcal |
| Protein | 3g |
| Carbs | 16g |
| Fat | 10g |
| Fibre | 5g |

*Source card: 150 kcal / 16C / 3P / 10F per serving.*

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes ~13 portions, 2.2×)
**Ingredients:**
- 9 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil (~135ml)
- 2 medium yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 1½–2 fennel bulbs, quartered + thinly sliced (optional — the author's signature addition; omit for cost)
- 4.4 lb (~2kg) sweet ripe bell peppers (red or red/yellow/orange mix — NOT green, too bitter), thinly sliced
- 4–5 garlic cloves, lightly crushed
- kosher salt
- 2¼ cups tomato sauce (blend crushed tomatoes, or use passata)
- 2¼ tbsp capers, rinsed + patted dry
- 4½ tbsp balsamic vinegar (or white balsamic)
- ½ cup torn fresh basil + mint

**Method:**
1. Cold pan: EVOO + onion + fennel + peppers + garlic; heat to medium; sauté 10 min, tossing.
2. Season (2 tsp salt); partially cover; 10–15 min until softened but with a little bite.
3. Add the tomato sauce + capers; cook uncovered ~10 min until reduced and silky.
4. Medium-high: add the balsamic; 1 min lively simmer, then 2–3 min low. Off heat: basil + mint. Rest 10 min; discard the garlic cloves; drizzle with EVOO.

**Storage:** Fridge 5 days — **better on day 2–3** (source). Serve warm, room temp or cold. Freezes acceptably (softer peppers on thaw).

#### Scaling Notes
- Scaled 2.2× (6 → ~13 side portions), providing 2 side buffers.
- **Fennel is the author's optional addition** — omit it for cost/simplicity (documented; it's not traditional anyway).
- Naturally GF; vegan side; no thickeners.

### 🥒 DISH 5: Zucchine Trifolate (Italian Sautéed Zucchini)
**Source:** https://www.lacucinaitaliana.com/italian-food/how-to-cook/love-zucchini-cook (La Cucina Italiana — the authority, since 1929)  
**Cross-check:** https://italianfoodforever.com/2018/07/zucchini-trifolati/ (garlic + parsley/mint version, serves 6)
**Why this recipe:** The simplest Italian side: zucchini + EVOO + garlic + parsley (or onion), sautéed hot-and-fast then finished gently. No liquid, no thickeners — naturally GF. Trifolati is a *method*, so the batch quantities below are the two sources' consensus.

**Original servings:** 4 | **Scaled to:** 14 portions (3.5×), 11 assigned + 3 buffer

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown
| Nutrient | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Calories | ~110 kcal |
| Protein | 2g |
| Carbs | 8g |
| Fat | 8g |
| Fibre | 2.5g |

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes ~14 portions, 3.5×)
**Ingredients:**
- 3.5kg zucchini (~13–15 medium or 7–8 very large), quartered lengthwise + cut into thick half-moons (seeds removed if watery — La Cucina's tip; save seeds for soup)
- 9 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1–2 large onions, finely chopped (La Cucina prefers onion + garlic) + 8–9 garlic cloves, slivered or minced
- 1 bunch fresh parsley, chopped (or basil/mint in season)
- kosher salt + black pepper
- *Optional (PBS variant):* 3–4 tomatoes, seeded + chopped, with the basil — a wetter, saucier version

**Method:**
1. Sauté the onion in the EVOO over medium-high ~1–2 min (Food Network timing); add the zucchini in a single layer (work in batches — do not crowd); sauté until golden and just soft, 8–10 min, stirring.
2. Add the garlic for the last 1–2 min (don't brown it).
3. Off heat: salt, pepper + a big shower of parsley. Serve warm or room temp. **No water added** — the zucchini releases its own (La Cucina's explicit instruction).

**Storage:** Fridge 4 days. Freezes acceptably (softens — fine for Mon–Tue). Reheat quickly in a skillet or microwave.

#### Scaling Notes
- Scaled 3.5× on the zucchini (a method dish — quantities from the two cross-check sources' consensus), providing 3 side buffers.
- Sauté in batches so the zucchini browns instead of steaming.
- Naturally GF; vegan.

### 🍝 GF PASTA — carb (all plates)
**Product:** Barilla Gluten-Free Spaghetti or Penne (certified GF; corn+rice blend) — stocked at Walmart Playa (⚠️ verify at the shop; if out, any GF-labelled pasta works, or fall back to baked polenta with pantry cornmeal).

Per portion (~150g cooked / ~60g dry): **~214 kcal / 4P / 47C / 1F.**

#### Batch Recipe (27 portions)
- **At least 1.7kg dry GF pasta total** (for example, 5 × 340g Barilla GF boxes or 4 × 454g boxes; pack sizes vary by store)
- Salt the water well (1 tbsp per pot)
- Cook al dente per package (GF pasta ~8–10 min, slightly shorter than wheat); **reserve pasta water** before draining (the bolognese + pomodoro sauces both use it); do not rinse.
- Toss each portion with its sauce (bolognese / pomodoro) 1–2 min — PWWB's "never plop sauce on pasta" rule — or portion pasta and sauce separately for packing (sauce absorbs into the pasta overnight either way).

**Storage:** Fridge 4 days. Freezes acceptably for Mon–Tue (2–4 day short freeze; softer on thaw). **Fallback:** a fresh 10-min pot on Monday night for Tuesday's plates if the texture bothers — GF pasta cooks fast.

#### Scaling Notes
- GF pasta absorbs sauce and softens faster than wheat — undercook by 1 minute for batch (the reheat finishes it).
- Parmesan: grate from a block for better flavour and melting; cellulose itself is GF, but check the full label if using pre-grated.

### ⚠️ NOTES & CONSIDERATIONS

- **GF handling this week:** every sauce is naturally gluten-free; the two watch-points are the **GF pasta product** (Barilla GF — check the label + shelf at Walmart; polenta fallback from pantry cornmeal if out of stock) and the **cacciatore dredge** (cornstarch replaces the flour — ¼ the quantity, dust lightly).

- **Shrimp:** Fri/Sat fresh-only (rubbery when frozen — same warning as the camarones week). The pomodoro sauce can be doubled and frozen in a future batch; this week's quantities are not doubled.
- **GF pasta freeze caveat:** Mon–Tue pasta is softer after the short freeze; sauce absorbs into it and it's fine — fallback is a fresh 10-minute pot on Monday.
