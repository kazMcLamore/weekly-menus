### 🍗 DISH 1: Lower-Salt Chopped Carolina Pork
**Source:** https://www.tasteofhome.com/recipes/carolina-style-pork-barbecue/  
**Cross-check:** https://heygrillhey.com/carolina-style-pulled-pork/  
**Why this recipe:** Taste of Home’s Test Kitchen recipe has 27 reviews and publishes both slow-cooker and covered Dutch-oven instructions. Hey Grill Hey (4.97/5, 32 votes) independently confirms pork shoulder, low-and-slow cooking, and a vinegar-pepper Carolina profile. This repeats the chopped barbecue Kaz liked while making only his requested salt changes.
**Original servings:** 14 small sandwich portions | **Scaled to:** Full source batch, repartitioned into 9 meal-prep portions

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown
| Nutrient | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Calories | 380 kcal |
| Protein | 28g (30%) |
| Carbs | 8g (8%) |
| Fat | 26g (62%) |
| Fibre | 1g |

#### Macro Calculation Summary
Nutrition database: [USDA FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/). Cooked pork shoulder, lean and fat (120g) ≈ 350 kcal/28P/0C/26F. Counting **all** onion and sugar in the source batch across 9 portions adds a conservative ~30 kcal/8C, even though some remains in discarded liquid. Macro-derived energy: 28×4 + 8×4 + 26×9 = 378 kcal, rounded to 380.

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes 9 portions)
**Ingredients:**
- 1.8kg / 4lb boneless pork shoulder butt / **espaldilla de cerdo**
- 2 Tbsp brown sugar
- **1 tsp kosher salt** (source: 2 tsp)
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 2 medium onions, quartered
- 3/4 cup / 180ml apple-cider vinegar
- 4 tsp GF Worcestershire sauce
- 1 Tbsp sugar
- 1 Tbsp crushed red-pepper flakes
- **1 tsp garlic powder** (source: garlic salt)
- 1 tsp ground mustard
- 1/2 tsp cayenne

**Method:**
1. Cut pork into four large pieces. Mix brown sugar, salt, paprika, and pepper; rub over pork. Put pork and onions in a covered Dutch oven.
2. Whisk vinegar, Worcestershire, sugar, pepper flakes, garlic powder, mustard, and cayenne; pour over the pork.
3. Cover tightly and cook at 150°C / 300°F for 3–4 hours, until fork-tender. Check at 3 hours; continue until it pulls apart without resistance.
4. Rest 15 minutes. Reserve up to 1 1/2 cups cooking liquid, skim its fat, and discard excess liquid. Shred with forks, then chop coarsely with a knife. Return only enough defatted liquid to keep the pork juicy.
5. Weigh cooked pork and divide evenly into 9 containers.

**Storage:** Refrigerate up to 4 days; freeze up to 3 months. Reheat covered with a spoonful of reserved liquid to 74°C / 165°F.

#### Scaling Notes
The ingredient quantities remain the source’s full 4lb recipe. Only two seasoning changes were made: salt reduced by half and garlic salt replaced 1:1 with garlic powder. Chopping after shredding changes texture, not formulation. Salt was not scaled linearly because Kaz specifically requested less.

### 🍗 DISH 2: Lower-Salt Memphis Oven Ribs
**Source:** https://barefeetinthekitchen.com/memphis-style-dry-ribs-recipe/
**Cross-check:** https://www.recipetineats.com/oven-baked-barbecue-pork-ribs/
**Why this recipe:** Barefeet in the Kitchen’s oven-native recipe is rated 4.84/5 and was the rib recipe used in Kaz’s earlier Southern week. It supplies a complete dry rub, vinegar dip, and tested 250°F oven method. RecipeTin Eats (4.98/5 from 275 votes) corroborates membrane removal and low oven cooking, although its ribs use a wet glaze.
**Original servings:** 5 | **Scaled to:** Full source batch, repartitioned into 8 portions

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown
| Nutrient | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Calories | 361 kcal |
| Protein | 28g (31%) |
| Carbs | 9g (10%) |
| Fat | 24g (60%) |
| Fibre | 1g |

#### Macro Calculation Summary
The fetched source card reports 577 kcal/44P/14C/38F for each of 5 servings. Dividing the same complete batch into 8 portions gives 577×5÷8 = 361 kcal; protein 44×5÷8 = 27.5g; carbs 8.75g; fat 23.75g. USDA roasted pork back-rib data (248 kcal/85g before rub) supports the order of magnitude.

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes 8 portions)
**Ingredients:**
- 1 slab pork loin spare/baby-back ribs, about 1.8kg / 4lb
- 5 Tbsp light brown sugar
- 4 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- **2 tsp kosher salt** (source: 4 tsp)
- 1/2 tsp chilli powder
- 1/4 tsp each onion powder, cumin, oregano, rubbed sage, marjoram, dried parsley, and white pepper
- 1 cup / 240ml white vinegar
- 1 cup / 240ml water

**Method:**
1. Put the oven rack in the middle and heat oven to 121°C / 250°F. Mix sugar and all spices. Reserve one-third in a separate bowl.
2. Put ribs meat-side down. Loosen the membrane with a knife, grip it with paper towel, and pull it off. Pat ribs dry.
3. Coat both sides with the remaining two-thirds rub. Place meat-side up on a foil-lined tray and bake uncovered about 3 hours, until tender.
4. Mix vinegar and water in a shallow tray. Briefly dip the cooked rack, place it on a clean board, and immediately apply the reserved rub. Rest 10 minutes, then cut between bones.
5. Weigh/portion as evenly as bone sizes allow; serve the two table sauces separately.

**Storage:** Refrigerate 3–4 days; freeze designated portions Friday. Reheat covered at 150°C / 300°F with a splash of water, then uncover briefly.

#### Scaling Notes
This is the source’s complete one-rack recipe, divided into 8 smaller meal-prep portions. The only formula change is reducing salt from 4 tsp to 2 tsp; a source reviewer specifically reported the original was too salty and recommended reducing it.

### 🍗 DISH 3: Oven-Baked BBQ Chicken
**Source:** https://www.recipetineats.com/oven-baked-bbq-chicken/  
**Cross-check:** https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/trisha-yearwood/oven-bbq-chicken-4697852
**Why this recipe:** RecipeTin Eats’ recipe is rated 4.95/5 from 37 votes and uses a tested homemade sauce with bone-in chicken. Trisha Yearwood’s 4.3/5, 83-review recipe independently confirms the covered/sauced dark-meat oven technique. This is the leanest of the three proteins.
**Original servings:** 5–6 | **Scaled to:** 8 portions (1 1/3× the six-serving recipe)

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown
| Nutrient | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Calories | 392 kcal |
| Protein | 33g (35%) |
| Carbs | 24g (25%) |
| Fat | 17g (40%) |
| Fibre | 1g |

#### Macro Calculation Summary
The fetched source card reports 392 kcal/33P/24C/17F per 256g serving, assuming excess fat is skimmed and the sauce is consumed. The recipe is scaled proportionally to 8 equal servings, so per-serving macros remain unchanged. Ingredient-level values were checked against USDA chicken, ketchup, sugar, and vinegar data; the source total is retained because it includes the tested recipe’s rendered-fat assumption.

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes 8 portions)
**Ingredients:**
- 2kg bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs/drumsticks
- 1 1/3 cups / 320ml GF ketchup
- 1/3 cup / 80ml apple-cider vinegar
- 2 2/3 tsp GF Worcestershire sauce
- 5 1/3 Tbsp / 67g brown sugar
- 2 2/3 tsp mustard powder
- 2 2/3 tsp onion powder
- **1/3 tsp salt** (half the linear scale)
- 2/3 tsp black pepper
- 1 1/3 cups / 320ml water

**Method:**
1. Mix the full sauce in a clean bowl. Before any raw chicken touches it, divide it **1:3** into two clean containers. Refrigerate the larger three-quarter portion covered for Friday.
2. **Wednesday:** Put 0.5kg chicken and the one-quarter sauce portion in a glass/ceramic dish, skin-side down. Bake at 200°C / 390°F for 20 minutes; turn, spoon over sauce, and bake 30 minutes more.
3. **Friday:** Repeat with 1.5kg chicken and the clean three-quarter sauce portion. Do not crowd the baking dish; use two dishes if necessary.
4. For both batches, check the thickest piece reaches at least 74°C / 165°F. Skim excess fat and broil 2–3 minutes to caramelise, watching constantly. Divide all chicken and sauce into 8 equal portions total.

**Storage:** Refrigerate 3–4 days; freeze next-week portions Friday. Reheat covered to 74°C / 165°F. The skin will soften after freezing.

#### Scaling Notes
All ingredients are scaled 1 1/3× from the published six-serving quantity; salt alone is held to half the linear amount. The recipe is split into 2 Wednesday portions and 6 Friday portions so Wednesday has two distinct meats while the majority of chicken is still cooked fresh Friday. Dividing sauce before raw-chicken contact preserves food safety and the source formula.

**Raw-chicken route:** Immediately after Tuesday shopping, refrigerate the 0.5kg Wednesday portion and freeze the sealed 1.5kg Friday portion. Move the frozen portion to the refrigerator **Thursday morning** to thaw safely for Friday cooking; never thaw on the counter.

### 🥬 DISH 4: Extra-Large Sweet-and-Tangy Collard Greens
**Source:** https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/sweet-and-tangy-collard-greens
**Cross-check:** https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/food-network-kitchen/collard-greens-recipe-2108010
**Why this recipe:** Bon Appétit’s Julia Sullivan recipe is rated 4.7/5 and uses optional bacon, water or low-sodium stock, and final seasoning—ideal for controlling salt. Food Network’s healthy collards independently confirm the onion-and-liquid braising method and a low-sodium result. This is 3.125× the published batch so every Southern plate gets a generous serving.
**Original servings:** 8 | **Scaled to:** 25 portions

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown

|----------|--------|

| Protein | 5g (17%) |
| Carbs | 11g (37%) |
| Fat | 6g (46%) |

#### Macro Calculation Summary
USDA ingredient totals divided by 25: 4.25kg raw collards ≈ 54 kcal/5P/9C/1F per portion; olive oil ≈ 45 kcal/5F; onion and sugar ≈ 19 kcal/2C. Total = 5×4 + 11×4 + 6×9 = 118 kcal. Water contributes zero; bacon is an optional source ingredient and is omitted.

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes 25 portions)
**Ingredients:**
- 9 Tbsp + 1 tsp / about 140ml extra-virgin olive oil
- 3 large onions, finely chopped
- 13 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 3 1/8 tsp crushed red-pepper flakes
- 4.25kg / 9.4lb collard greens, stems removed, leaves cut into 2.5cm strips
- 9 3/8 cups / 2.2L water (or labelled-GF unsalted stock)
- 185ml / scant 3/4 cup + 1 Tbsp apple-cider vinegar
- 2 Tbsp + 1/4 tsp sugar
- Black pepper; salt only after tasting, if needed

**Method:**
1. Use two large pots and divide all ingredients evenly. Heat oil; cook onion 5–7 minutes. Add garlic and pepper flakes for 30 seconds.
2. Add greens by handfuls, letting each batch wilt before adding more. Add water; simmer and stir about 4 minutes until volume drops by half.
3. Reduce to medium-low, partially cover, and simmer 25–30 minutes until tender and most liquid evaporates.
4. Stir in vinegar and sugar; cook 1 minute. Add pepper. Taste before adding any salt.
5. Divide into 25 generous portions.

**Storage:** Refrigerate 3–4 days; freeze designated portions with their liquid. Reheat gently by microwave or stovetop.

#### Scaling Notes
This is a 3.125× scale with practical rounding under 4%. Bacon was optional in the published recipe and is omitted. Salt is not scaled because reducing 2.2L of liquid can concentrate it; use water and season only at the end. Two pots prevent steaming/crowding problems.

### 🍞 DISH 5: Flourless Gluten-Free Cornbread
**Source:** https://whatgreatgrandmaate.com/gluten-free-cornbread-no-flour/  
**Cross-check:** https://glutenfreeonashoestring.com/old-fashioned-gluten-free-cornbread/
**Why this recipe:** What Great Grandma Ate’s cornmeal-only recipe is rated 5/5 from 16 votes, yields 16 pieces from one pan, and avoids specialty GF flour. Gluten Free on a Shoestring (5/5 from 855 votes) confirms the yogurt, cornmeal, egg, honey, and butter structure.
**Original servings:** 16 | **Scaled to:** 16 portions (unchanged)

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown

|----------|--------|
| Calories | 136 kcal |
| Protein | 3g (9%) |
| Carbs | 20g (58%) |
| Fat | 5g (33%) |

#### Macro Calculation Summary
The fetched source card reports 136 kcal/3P/20C/5F/2 fibre per one-sixteenth pan. Macro-derived energy is 3×4 + 20×4 + 5×9 = 137 kcal. USDA cornmeal, yogurt, butter, honey, and egg values reproduce this within rounding.

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes 16 portions)
**Ingredients:**
- 2 cups / about 300g certified-GF medium/coarse cornmeal
- 2 tsp GF baking powder
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 1/2 cups / 360g plain yogurt or sour cream
- 1/4 cup / 60ml honey
- 1/4 cup / 56g melted unsalted butter or ghee

- 1 tsp vanilla

**Method:**
1. Heat oven to 190°C / 375°F. Grease/line a 20cm / 8-inch square pan.

4. Cool 10 minutes and cut into 16 equal pieces.

**Storage:** Airtight in fridge up to 1 week; freeze up to 3 months. Reheat covered for about 20 seconds in the microwave.

#### Scaling Notes
No scaling or ingredient modification. Use certified-GF cornmeal: not masa harina and not cornstarch.

### 🥔 DISH 6: Creamy Mashed Potatoes
**Source:** https://www.simplyrecipes.com/recipes/perfect_mashed_potatoes/
**Cross-check:** https://www.recipetineats.com/mashed-potato/
**Why this recipe:** Simply Recipes uses Yukon Gold potatoes with warmed cream, butter, a small amount of milk, and measured seasoning. RecipeTin Eats (4.97/5 from 31 ratings) confirms draining thoroughly, warming dairy, and avoiding machines that make potatoes gluey.
**Original servings:** 4 | **Scaled to:** 2× recipe, divided into 11 smaller portions

#### Per-Portion Macro Breakdown
| Nutrient | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Calories | 175 kcal |
| Protein | 3g (7%) |
| Carbs | 22g (51%) |
| Fat | 8g (42%) |
| Fibre | 3g |

#### Macro Calculation Summary
USDA ingredient total for 1.36kg Yukon Gold potatoes, 120ml heavy cream, 60g butter, and 30ml milk is about 1,920 kcal. Dividing into 11 gives ~175 kcal/3P/22C/8F. Macro-derived energy is 172 kcal; database and rounding explain the small difference.

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes 11 portions)
**Ingredients:**
- 1.36kg / 3lb Yukon Gold potatoes, peeled and quartered
- 120ml / 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 60g / 4 Tbsp unsalted butter
- 30ml / 2 Tbsp milk, plus more only if needed
- 1 tsp salt for the cooking water
- 1 tsp salt for the mash; black pepper to taste

**Method:**
1. Cover potatoes with cold water and 1 tsp salt. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, and simmer until completely tender.
2. Drain very well. Warm cream and butter together without boiling.
3. Mash potatoes by hand, gradually incorporating warm dairy and milk. Add the remaining 1 tsp salt and black pepper.
4. Divide by weight into 11 containers. Never use a blender or food processor.

**Storage:** Refrigerate 3–4 days; freeze designated portions. Reheat covered with a teaspoon of milk/cream.

#### Scaling Notes
The current source formula is doubled exactly, including milk and its two measured salt additions, then divided into 11 rather than 8 smaller portions to keep chicken plates below 700 kcal. Weighing and container division are BatchCraft packing steps, not source instructions.

#### Scaled Batch Recipe (Makes 480ml Carolina + 600ml Kansas City)
**Carolina ingredients:**
- 2 cups / 480ml apple-cider vinegar
- 2 Tbsp / 15g coarsely ground black pepper
- 1 1/2 Tbsp / 20g brown sugar

**Kansas City ingredients:**
